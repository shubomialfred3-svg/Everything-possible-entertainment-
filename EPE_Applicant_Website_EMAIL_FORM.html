Help

Applicant FAQ

Are applications open?

No. This is a website prototype. Real applications must wait until secure backend services and adult supervision are in place.

Who can apply?

The planned age range is 9–15. Country eligibility will be confirmed before launch.

Can I apply again?

The planned applicant system will support re-auditions through the same account, retaining previous attempts privately.

Will my video be public?

No. Audition videos are intended to remain private. Any separate public use requires specific guardian consent and the young person's agreement.
